Why this site exists

Our planet is facing big problems like climate change, pollution and loss of wildlife. This website is about those problems and also about simple things normal people can do to help protect the Earth.

Climate change

Climate change means the Earth is getting warmer because of gases from cars, factories and burning fossil fuels. This can cause stronger storms, rising sea levels and hotter summers.

Pollution

Pollution is when harmful things go into the air, water or land. Plastic in the ocean, dirty smoke from cars and litter in parks are all examples of pollution.

Simple ways you can help

  • Turn off lights when you leave a room
  • Use a reusable water bottle
  • Walk or cycle for short trips
  • Recycle paper, plastic and glass
  • Plant a tree if you can
