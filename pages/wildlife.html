Why wildlife matters

Animals, birds, fish and insects all help keep nature in balance. When forests are cut down or rivers get dirty, many animals lose their homes. Protecting wildlife also helps people, because we need healthy nature for food, clean air and clean water.

Problems animals face

  1. Loss of forests and natural homes
  2. Plastic and other pollution
  3. Hunting and illegal trade
  4. Climate change changing the weather

How we can protect wildlife

Support nature parks, stop littering, and choose products that do not harm animals. Even small actions at home can help wildlife around the world.
